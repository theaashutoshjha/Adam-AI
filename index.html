    Call compliance and acceptable use

    Rules for customers using AI Support Desk and AI Audit

    AI Support Desk: outbound and inbound calls

    The customer is the principal entity responsible for every call placed under its registration. Before you call, make sure you:

    • are registered under TRAI’s TCCCPR 2018 framework (DLT) where your calls are commercial communications, and use only approved headers and templates;
    • check numbers against the national do-not-call preference register and hold valid consent where required;
    • call only within permitted hours and show a valid caller ID;
    • tell people at the start that they are speaking with an AI agent and that the call may be recorded. A TRAI proposal on AI voice is expected to make this disclosure mandatory, and we recommend it now;
    • keep records of consent, call outcome and recording notices.

    AI Audit: recordings

    You must have a lawful basis and any required notice or consent for the recordings you upload. If you are regulated, for example as a SEBI-registered intermediary, you remain responsible for your own recording, retention and reporting duties. ADAM-AR assists with audit and does not replace them.

    Not allowed

    • Spam, harassment, threats or misleading calls.
    • Impersonating a person or organisation, or cloning a voice without permission.
    • Fraud, unlawful collections practices or any unlawful content.
    • Trying to bypass limits, security or other customers’ isolation.

    We may suspend a service that breaks these rules or that telecom providers or regulators ask us to stop.
